01 / CONTEXT

The through-line

Software is the instrument. Curiosity is the direction.

I’m interested in the space where artificial systems start asking questions that used to belong to biology: how do we learn, represent the world, adapt, remember, predict and make decisions?

My current toolkit spans full-stack development, Python, AI, cybersecurity, web systems, databases, automation and hardware experimentation. The longer-term destination is computational neuroscience: using computation as a way to understand intelligence itself.
